# Monica Rust privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

Monica Rust stores your local account, people, notes, imported records, preferences, and downloaded media in the application data on your Mac. The app uses the email address you enter for local sign-in. Adding a local account does not register a developer-hosted account.

You choose whether to import data and connect a source. An optional connector may contact its source service to sign in, load records, or fetch media. The source service processes those requests under its own privacy policy. Imported records may contain other people's data. Only provide data you have permission to use.

Model features are optional. If you configure and use a model service, Monica sends the request and any workspace context needed for that feature to the endpoint you selected. Your selected provider controls how it processes and retains that data. For requests with sensitive information, choose a provider whose policy fits your needs. The project's developer does not receive the contents of your local database through the app itself.

Connector controls let you pause or disable future collection. Local data you already saved remains in the app until you remove it. To remove the local database, close Monica Rust and remove its app data from your Mac. If you have copies or backups, manage those independently. If you contacted a source service or model provider, use its account or privacy controls for data it holds.

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to share and uses it to respond. Contact [Monica Rust support](mailto:max@12450.top) for privacy questions or requests. For general questions, see [support](macos-support.md).
