# Monica Rust privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

The Mac App Store edition of Monica Rust stores contacts, notes, and interaction history in the app's private storage on your Mac. It functions offline without an account. The app does not send your workspace or usage data to the developer, connect to online services, or include analytics or advertising SDKs.

When you import, Monica Rust reads the file you select through the macOS file picker and copies the contact information you approve into the workspace. The original file stays where it is. If you export a backup, macOS asks where to save it. That backup contains the workspace in readable JSON. Keep it somewhere you trust.

You can remove a contact and its history from the workspace using the contact's action menu. Copies you exported are managed separately. If you contact support, the developer uses what you choose to share to answer your request. Email [Monica Rust support](mailto:max@12450.top) for privacy questions.
