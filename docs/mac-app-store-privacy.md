# Monica Rust Mac App Store privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

The Mac App Store edition stores your local sign-in, contacts, notes, interactions, and preferences inside the app’s sandbox on your Mac. Its user interface talks to an app-owned service on your Mac over a loopback connection. Creating a local account does not register an account with the developer.

For CSV import, Monica Rust reads the file you select and shows a preview. When you confirm the import, it copies those selected records into the local workspace. The original file stays where it is.

This edition does not connect to social platforms or model services. It does not send workspace information to the developer or use advertising or analytics SDKs. If you email support, the developer uses the information you choose to share to answer your request.

Contact [Monica Rust support](mailto:max@12450.top) for privacy questions. See [support](mac-app-store-support.md) for help with the app.
